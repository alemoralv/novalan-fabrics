Stage

Spinning

Under a strict quality control, our spinning mills have the capacity to supply not just 100% of our yarn needs, but also allow us to have presence in the national and international yarn market.

Our main raw materials are: wool and its blends with other animal-origin fibers like cashmere, mohair, camel hair and silk; natural fibers like linen and cotton; as well as synthetic fibers like polyester, nylon, aramids and viscose.

Stage

Weaving

We produce fabrics with technology, dedication and diligent care: avant-garde weavings with different natural and artificial fibers.

We offer a broad variety of weights, constructions and finishing effects, also developing special weaving structures and finishing processes according to the customer's needs.

Stage

Dyeing and Finishing

The dyes and chemical products we use are elaborated by companies recognized worldwide, which endorse and advise us on optimizing the dyeing and finishing processes.

All those dyes and chemical products fulfill international quality and environmental standards.

We can dye at any stage of production: fiber, top, yarn and fabric, under a strict quality system and supervision.

Cutting-edge technology allows us to dye and finish all types of natural, synthetic and artificial fibers, regular and consistent in the color.

Stage

Laboratory

All of our processes are perfectly controlled and monitored from beginning to end by our quality control laboratories.

We assure the quality of our products through different tests: stable finishing tones, fastness to light and washing, density, shrinkage, and more.

Stage

Design

Our service combines the broad experience of our personnel with the use of cutting-edge technology.

Our collections are present at the most important international exhibitions and trade shows, keeping us at the forefront of fashion tendencies.
